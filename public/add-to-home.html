Add it to your home screen

Posh Services will sit on your phone like an app and open full screen, ready for the next trip. It takes about ten seconds. No app store, nothing to pay.

You're already using it from your home screen. Splendid.

  1. Open Posh Services in SafariGo to poshservices.carlosfandango.net. (In Chrome on an iPhone, the Share button is at the top right instead.)
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen ⬆︎.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen"Scroll down the list if you can't see it, then tap Add in the top corner.
  1. Open Posh Services in ChromeGo to poshservices.carlosfandango.net.
  2. Tap the menuThe three dots ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"Then tap Add or Install.
  1. In Chrome or EdgeLook for the little install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose Install Posh Services.
  2. In Safari on a MacChoose File then Add to Dock.
  3. Or just bookmark itOld-fashioned, reliable, like a Thermos.

Once it's on your home screen, I'll be right there whenever you need a decent flapjack. I promise not to send notifications. I wouldn't know how.
